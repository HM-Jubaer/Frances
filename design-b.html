03Perspective

Most businesses are not broken. They are compensating.

Most problems in a growing business are not failures of effort or intent. They are the visible signs of systems built for an earlier version of the business, now asked to do more than they were built for.

Frances works where strategy, execution and financial performance meet. She starts with how decisions actually move, where accountability lives in practice rather than on the org chart, and what the business has stopped telling its leaders.

What comes out is clarity, sequenced action, and a foundation for performance that holds.

  1. Where growth exposed strain that was always there
  2. Where execution became uneven despite good people
  3. Where profit slipped without an obvious cause in the numbers
  4. Where a worsening customer experience is an operating signal
  5. Where dependence on the founder became a ceiling
  6. Where a new market showed the operating model does not travel
  7. Where pressure on leaders became normal and stopped being treated as information

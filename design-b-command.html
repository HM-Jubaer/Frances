Perspective

Businesses are not broken. They are compensating.

Most problems in a growing business are visible signs of systems built for an earlier version of the business, now asked to do more than they were built for.

Frances starts with how decisions actually move, where accountability lives in practice rather than on the org chart, and what the business has stopped telling its leaders.

The result is not a transformation program. It is clarity, sequenced action, and a foundation for performance that holds.

How do I find out where my business is actually losing profitability?

The Profit Pulse™ shows where profitability is leaking, what is driving the gap between effort and financial outcome, and what to address first for the highest return. It is built for business owners who know something is off but cannot locate it precisely.

Why does my business feel harder to run even though revenue keeps growing?

Most problems in a growing business are not failures of effort or intent. They are the visible signs of systems built for an earlier version of the business, now asked to do more than they were built for.

How is working with Frances different from a typical consultant?

Frances has operated inside businesses at scale, across industries, geographies and conditions that most advisors encounter only from the outside. The result is clarity, sequenced action, and a structural foundation for performance that holds.

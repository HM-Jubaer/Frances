Perspective

Businesses are not broken. They are compensating.

Most problems in a growing business are not failures of effort or intent. They are the visible signs of systems built for an earlier version of the business, now asked to do more than they were built for.

Frances works where strategy, execution, and financial performance meet. She starts with how decisions actually move, and where accountability lives in practice rather than on the org chart.

The result is not a transformation program. It is clarity, sequenced action, and a foundation for performance that holds.

She looks for:

  • Where growth exposed strain that was always there but hidden at a smaller size
  • Where execution became uneven despite good people and clear intentions
  • Where profit slipped without an obvious cause in the numbers
  • Where a worsening customer experience is an operating signal, not a service problem
  • Where dependence on the founder became a limit on what the business can become
  • Where expanding into new markets showed the operating model does not travel
  • Where pressure on leaders became normal and stopped being treated as information

06Imagery

A diamond frame, as in Design 1.

The hero portrait sits in a rotated rounded square, with floating cards overlapping its edges. Other photos use rounded rectangles (26px).

  • Use a hi-res brand shoot of at least 2000px. The current 480px portrait is a placeholder.
  • Use a plain light background, so the photo can be cut out later for a Design 1-style layered hero.
  • Wardrobe in navy, white or soft neutrals, which work with the lime accent.
  • Shoot real work moments: whiteboard sessions, reviewing numbers, client conversations.

08Rules

Keep it fresh.

  • One primary action per view, and lime marks Profit Pulse™
  • Generous white space inside rounded frames
  • Real figures only: 30+, 3, 6 and $750
  • Short, plain headlines in Frances's voice
  • No Source Serif or PT Sans, which belong to the current site
  • No fabricated metrics, ratings or client logos
  • No gradients or glows. Use flat colour and soft shadow
  • No more than one lime element competing per screen

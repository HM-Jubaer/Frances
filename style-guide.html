01

Design principles

The brand is a senior operator rather than a motivational coach. The design should feel calm, precise and expensive, in the way a Swiss private bank does, not a SaaS startup.

1

Quiet authority

Use generous whitespace and a serif voice. Let the copy do the work, with no gimmicks.

2

One clear door

Profit Pulse™ is the primary action on every screen. Booking a conversation is secondary.

3

Signal, not noise

The "pulse line" motif shows diagnosis: reading the system's signals.

4

Proof over praise

Name the companies, years and continents. Use concrete numbers wherever they exist.

Do

  • Use peach on navy for the main CTA (high contrast)
  • Use about 60% paper/ivory, 30% navy and 10% peach
  • Keep red for labels and the "Critical" risk level

Don't

  • Don't set peach text on white for body copy (fails AA). Use Peach Dark instead
  • Don't use more than one peach CTA per viewport
  • Don't add new hues outside this palette

The Profit Pulse™ system

This is how Profit Pulse gets attention. It is a visual language reserved for the diagnostic, so it is recognisable anywhere on the site.

Profit Pulse™

The pulse line is the signature motif. Use it in the hero, as section dividers and on the scan CTA. Animate it once on view, or loop it subtly.

  1. Nav CTA (sticky, peach)
  2. Hero primary button
  3. Hero floating risk-profile card
  4. Dedicated feature section

Imagery

Currently there is one 480px portrait. That is the biggest visual gap.

Portrait treatment: an arch or soft blob mask, never a hard square.

Photography brief

  • • A hi-res brand shoot (minimum 2000px), in navy, teal and coral wardrobe
  • • Working shots: whiteboard, client table, reviewing numbers
  • • Swiss and European environmental shots (Lake Geneva light)
  • • Natural, warm grade. No stock "handshake" photos

Decorative

  • • Pulse line (signature)
  • • Hatched circles (Avantage reference), in peach at 30% opacity
  • • Thin navy rules and serif numerals 01 / 02 / 03

09

Voice & copy

Keep Frances's voice as it is: declarative, diagnostic and calm. Shorten it for scanning.

Sounds like Frances

  • "Most businesses are not broken. They are compensating."
  • "A first conversation is for understanding, not selling."
  • "No cash. No credit card. No obligation."

Avoid

  • Hype: "10x your profits!", "game-changing"
  • Generic consultant phrases: "innovative solutions"
  • Exclamation marks and emoji
